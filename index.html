My Engineering Philosophy

Good ML doesn't ignore domain knowledge — it encodes it. Whether it's power system dynamics or battery electrochemistry, embedding physical laws into the loss function makes models robust even with limited data.
A model that predicts accurately but can't be explained is a liability in high-stakes settings. I gravitate toward SHAP, spectral analysis, and Koopman methods that let you see inside the decision.
From SCRUM sprints at DriverAI to full-stack ML pipelines, I think in terms of components, interfaces, and feedback loops. Building the model is 20% of the work. Building the system around it is the other 80%.
